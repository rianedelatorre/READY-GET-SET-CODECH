PRIVACY POLICY

Data Collection & Email Orders

We collect essential customer information—such as your full name, email address, shirt size preference, and shipping details—only when you submit an inquiry or place an order via our direct email draft system. We do not store financial or credit card information on this website.

Use of Information

Customer details are strictly used for order processing, size confirmation, and delivery coordination. Dash Apparel respects your privacy and will never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses minimal, essential cookies necessary for website navigation and basic layout preferences. We do not use invasive third-party tracking scripts or user-profiling cookies.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Order & Pricing Terms

All t-shirt prices are listed in Philippine Pesos (₱). Clicking the "ORDER NOW" button creates a direct email draft. Submitting this email serves as an official order inquiry; order status, payment instructions, and delivery details will be confirmed directly via email response from our team.

Please carefully review your shirt dimensions and select your preferred size option (S, M, L) on the Product Page before sending your order email. Because our items are produced in limited batches, size exchanges are subject to stock availability upon email inquiry.
